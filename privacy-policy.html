Privacy Policy – Cirasis

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in the mobile game Cirasis ("the Game"). The Game does not require an account, and we do not run our own servers or analytics. The only personal data processed in connection with the Game is processed by our advertising partners, as described below.

1. Who we are

The Game is developed and published by Marko Miettinen ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in Finland. Privacy contact: cirasisgame@gmail.com.

We decide which advertising software is included in the Game and are responsible for asking for your consent. We do not receive or store your personal data ourselves. Once data is sent to our advertising partners, they process it as independent controllers.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your settings and progress locally on your device: your privacy choices, control settings, difficulty and the checkpoints you have reached. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

3. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows ads. Ads are delivered through these partners:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation), operated by Unity Technologies – Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies – same privacy policy
  • Google AdMob, operated by Google – Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The advertising software in the Game may collect the following data from your device:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or region)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and the Game's version
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or clicked

This data is used to show ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform. If you accept personalized ads, it may also be used to show ads based on your interests. Unity and Google each process this data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. Within Unity's mediation, data may be shared with the other ad networks listed above when they compete to show an ad.

4. Your choice about personalized ads

When you start the Game, a privacy message from Google's consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform, which follows the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) asks for your choices where the law requires it, for example in the EU/EEA and the UK. Ads are loaded only after this message has been answered. If you do not consent, the Game still works and you may see limited ads that do not use your personal data for personalization. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time in SETTINGS > PRIVACY > OPTIONS.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device).

5. Legal bases (EU/EEA and UK)

  • Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for personalized ads and for storing or accessing information on your device for that purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time in SETTINGS; this does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), where our advertising partners rely on it, for non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and ad measurement. You have the right to object to this processing.

6. International transfers

Unity Technologies and Google are based in the United States and may process data outside the EU/EEA. They describe the safeguards they use for such transfers in their privacy policies.

7. Data retention and security

We do not collect or store personal data on our own systems. Data on your device is kept until you uninstall the Game or clear its app data. Our advertising partners keep data for as long as described in their privacy policies. We use established advertising partners and do not add any other third-party data collection to the Game.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the Game and their data has been processed for personalized ads, please contact us and we will help make the request to our advertising partners.

9. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled by Unity and Google (see their privacy policies). You can also contact us, and we will help.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Finland, this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or its partners change. The latest version is always available at this address, and the effective date above shows when it was last updated.
